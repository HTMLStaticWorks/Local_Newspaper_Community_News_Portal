Who We Are

The Voice of Local Journalism

We believe a neighborhood is only as strong as its common knowledge. Since 1994, our reporters have documented our town’s changes, councils, and stories.

Founded as a small, four-page physical flyer by local print technician Arthur Sterling, The NEWZ grew into a critical community pillar. Our mission remains simple: to deliver factual, rigorous, and relevant news that serves local families and holds representatives accountable.

Our History Timeline

Three decades of commitment to independent municipal reporting.

1994

The Heidelberg Setup

Arthur Sterling purchases a secondhand printing press, publishing a weekly 4-page civic newsletter to cover local municipal council boards.

2002

Daily Broadsheet Transition

The publication shifts from weekly flyers to a daily print format, adding a dedicated news desk and full-time sports columnists.

2011

Going Digital

The NEWZ launches its first web portal, offering regional news updates online and introducing classified community boards.

2026

Premium Magazine Relaunch

We launch our modern responsive layout system, integrating light/dark themes, podcasts, and automated localized ads.
